# A Call Before the Rain

## The first ring

Mara stopped under the porch light. The street had gone quiet.

Mara: Are you still there?

Ellen: Yes, dear. I found it. It was in your father's old coat all along.

Who could blame her for laughing? She had spent an hour looking in the wrong room.

Mara: Take your time. I'm here.

## After the call

The rain finally came. Mara stayed on the porch long enough to hear it settle into a steady rhythm.
